ANDROID CONNECTION

Sync sleep from Samsung Health

HEALTH CONNECT

Use the ZzzRise Android companion to securely read sleep sessions that Samsung Health shares with Android Health Connect. Sign in with your ZzzRise account, grant sleep-data permission, then sync. Your imported sessions will appear in your history and insights.

1Set up Health ConnectMake sure Samsung Health is syncing sleep data to Health Connect.
2Sign in to the companionUse your ZzzRise email and password. Your Samsung account is not used to sign in to ZzzRise.
3Allow sleep access and syncChoose to share sleep sessions. You can revoke access in Android settings.

Connect with a QR code

Enter your ZzzRise server address in the Android companion first (use your computer's local network address or your deployed HTTPS address). Then open ZzzRise Sync and tap Sign in with QR code. The code expires after five minutes and can only be used once.

Your QR code will appear here.

Scan inside the ZzzRise Sync Android app

No Android sleep sessions have been imported yet. The companion app is in the Android companion folder of this project.

Health Connect imports completed sleep sessions after Samsung Health has synced them. It does not infer sleep from a stationary phone, and sync may be delayed.
